02 — ENGINEERING SOLUTIONS & CONSULTING · INDUSTRIAL & UTILITIES

Process and utility engineering for plants that can't afford downtime.

We specify, source and support the equipment that moves, seals and controls fluid — plus the turnkey projects and utilities around it — across pharma, chemical, food and process industries. Specification-led, so you buy what the process actually needs, with the documentation regulated manufacturing demands.

  • Process equipment — reactors, isolators, dryers, pumps, valves and single-use fluid-path assemblies, specified to your process.
  • Turnkey & EPC support — design, supply, installation and commissioning, managed as one scope.
  • Utilities & fluid control — clean and process utilities, distribution, instrumentation and control.
  • Peristaltic pumping & transfer — accurate, low-shear transfer and feeding, automation-ready.
  • Validation & documentation — certificates of conformance, traceability and qualification support.
