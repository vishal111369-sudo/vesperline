Questions we hear first

Do we have to change our systems?
No. We work inside the platform, tools and licences you already have. Nothing is migrated and nothing new is installed unless you ask for it.
Who exactly does the work?
Named people you meet before we start. You know who runs your campaigns, who owns your integrations and who to call. The same people stay on your account.
How do you handle our data and access?
We use the access you grant, inside your systems, with named accounts and the permissions you set. Your data stays where it is and stays yours.
How fast can you start?
The free audit starts as soon as you ask for it. A pilot usually starts within two weeks of agreeing the scope.
What if it doesn't work?
That is what the pilot is for. It has one clear metric agreed up front, so we both know within weeks whether the work is paying for itself.

Tell us what the operation looks like now.

Thirty minutes, no slides. You describe the operation, we tell you honestly where we would start, or whether we are the wrong fit.
