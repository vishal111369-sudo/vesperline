Four modules, one team

Each module stands on its own, and each one is run by named people inside your systems. Start with one. Most teams add a second within a quarter.

Vesperline Run

The daily operation, run properly and on time, every day.

What we run

  • Planned and ad-hoc campaigns, built, checked and sent on schedule
  • Promotions and rewards set up with the right limits
  • Customer segments kept current
  • Verification and payout queues worked within agreed times
  • Content publishing and translations
  • Weekly management reporting

What you get each month

  • A weekly operations report
  • A change log of everything we touched
  • A calendar of what goes out next
  • Time spent on each kind of work

Where we work

Customer platformsCRM and lifecycle toolsService desksContent systemsWork management
Typical first 30 days. We document how the operation runs today, take over the repeatable work first, and agree turnaround times for every queue.

Vesperline Connect

Systems that agree with each other, and alerts before anything breaks quietly.

What we run

  • Integrations between your platform, CRM and service tools
  • Data sync checks and reconciliation (making sure two systems hold the same numbers)
  • Duplicate and broken record clean-up
  • Alerts for silent failures, sent within hours
  • Migration support when you change a tool

What you get each month

  • A data health report
  • An alert log, with what was fixed and when
  • One agreed count for your core numbers
  • A map of how your systems connect

Where we work

APIs and webhooksData warehousesReporting toolsMessaging providers
Typical first 30 days. We map every connection, find where the numbers disagree, and set up alerts on the ones that matter most.

Vesperline Automate

Repetitive work handed to automation and AI, safely.

What we run

  • Rule-based automation for repeat tasks
  • AI drafts for replies and content, checked by a person before anything is sent
  • Approval steps and full logs on every automated action
  • Test sets before any automation goes live
  • A way to reverse every change

What you get each month

  • Hours saved, by task
  • An automation register (a list of every automation, its owner and its last check)
  • Approval and error logs
  • A short list of what to automate next

Where we work

Your existing tools firstWorkflow automationAI models under your control
Typical first 30 days. We measure where the team spends its week, pick the three most repetitive tasks, and automate one with an approval step.

Vesperline Measure

Proof of what the work earned, not just what it touched.

What we run

  • A holdout group (a small random group left untouched) for overall impact
  • Control groups on individual campaigns
  • Net revenue tracked over 30 days, not clicks
  • Payback tracking for new customers by monthly group
  • A monthly proof report your finance team can check

What you get each month

  • Extra net revenue created
  • The cost of creating it
  • Return on that cost
  • Days for a new customer to pay back what they cost to acquire

Where we work

Your platform dataYour data warehouseSpreadsheets your finance team trusts
Typical first 30 days. We agree the method with you in writing, set up the untouched group, and send the first baseline report.
