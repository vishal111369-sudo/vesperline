Gaps, ranked by impact

GapWhy it mattersImpact
No message on day 1 after sign-upThe first contact arrives on day 3, after most new customers have already gone quiet.
Welcome offer sent to accounts already flagged as duplicatesBudget leaks to repeat sign-ups before any check runs.
Second-purchase journey missingNothing nudges a first-time buyer towards a second purchase in the first week.
Same message for every customerHigh and low value customers receive identical content and offers.
Push and email send at the same minuteCustomers get two copies of one message, and opt-outs rise.
Unsubscribe link fails on mobileCustomers who cannot opt out mark messages as spam instead.
No control group on any campaignNobody can say which messages actually changed behaviour.
Reward expiry not explainedCustomers find out at the point of use, which creates complaints.
Support replies take 26 hours at weekendsMost new customers sign up at weekends.
Active customer count differs across three reportsDecisions use whichever number is open at the time.
Inactive customers receive daily messagesFrequency is not reduced for people who stopped engaging.
Brand tone changes between channelsEmail sounds formal, push sounds casual, and the brand feels inconsistent.

Three journeys, rebuilt

Day 1 to 7 welcome

  • Day 0 welcome with one clear next step
  • Day 1 reminder if there is no first purchase
  • Day 3 help message with real support hours
  • Day 7 offer based on first activity

Second purchase

  • Starts 24 hours after the first purchase
  • Offer sized by first purchase value
  • Stops the moment the second purchase happens

Quiet customer

  • Starts after the customer's usual gap, not a fixed date
  • Frequency drops as engagement drops
  • Control group from the first send

The next ninety days

Days 1 to 30

  • Fix the three highest-impact gaps
  • Set up the untouched group
  • Agree one count of active customers

Days 31 to 60

  • Launch the rebuilt welcome journey
  • Add the second-purchase journey
  • First proof report

Days 61 to 90

  • Launch the quiet-customer journey
  • Cut messages that show no effect
  • Plan the next quarter from results
