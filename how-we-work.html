How we work

Clear steps, named people, and nothing that locks you in. Here is what working with us looks like, including when you should not hire us.

  1. A 30-minute call

    You describe the operation as it is today. We ask questions and say honestly whether we can help.

  2. A written proposal

    The scope, the people, the one metric we will be judged on, and how we will measure it.

  3. You meet your team

    The named people who will run your work, before anything starts.

  4. We run the work

    Inside your systems, with a weekly report and a change log of everything we touch.

  5. A monthly review

    What was done, what it earned compared with the untouched group, and what comes next.

Three ways to engage

Dedicated specialist

One named expert embedded in your team for a defined area.

Fits teams with a clear gap in one skill.

Dedicated team

A small named team covering operations, data and automation together.

Fits operations that are growing faster than hiring.

Managed function

We own an entire function end to end, against agreed results.

Fits leaders who want an outcome, not headcount.

What stays yours

  • Your platform. We work inside it. We never ask you to switch.
  • Your licences. Every tool stays in your name and on your contract.
  • Your data. It stays in your systems. We access it with named accounts you control.
  • Your playbooks. Everything we document is handed to you and stays with you.

When not to hire us

  • You need a new platform built from scratch.
  • You want the cheapest possible hours, with no ownership of results.
  • You are not ready to give access to the systems we would run.
  • You need a team on site every day.

More questions

What hours do you cover?
Your core business hours, with agreed cover for evenings and weekends where your customers are most active.
How do you keep our access secure?
Named accounts only, the permissions you choose, and a log of every change. Access is removed the same day an engagement ends.
How is the work priced?
By the people and time the work needs, agreed in the written proposal. You see exactly who you are paying for.
Can we start with one module?
Yes. Most clients start with one, usually Run or Measure, and add the next once they see results.
Do you replace our team?
No. We take the repeatable work off their plate so they can focus on decisions.
